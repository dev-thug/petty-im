# Petty hero copy

## Product grounding

Source app: `/Users/hyunjoong/petty` (read-only research).

- `README.md`: AI character conversations, roleplay and interactive stories are the product category.
- `features/welcome/welcome-content.ts`: established brand invitation is “당신만의 이야기”.
- `features/home/home-data.ts` and `features/home/presentation/HomeScreen.tsx`: browse characters/stories across romance, fantasy, modern, SF and everyday-life genres.
- `features/create/create-data.ts`: users can create story flows, characters and worldbooks.
- `docs/AGENT.md` §11: conversation history and extracted memory facts have bounded retention. This supports continuity, not an absolute claim of remembering everything forever.
- `docs/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`: inviting, polite language; avoid promises of unavailable functionality.

## Positioning decision

Audience: people seeking character-led roleplay and stories they can participate in.
Promise: meet an imagined character and take the protagonist's role through conversation.
Proof in the hero: explicitly name AI characters and conversational story creation.
Tone: warm, imaginative, invitational; align with the supplied night-sky design.

A separately named brand-marketing skill was not installed. Used the available Creative Production positioning guide's audience / occasion / proof / differentiation framework as supporting guidance; no visual-generation board was needed for this text-only change.

## Applied copy

Headline:

> 상상 속 캐릭터와
> 내가 주인공이 되는
> 이야기, Petty

Description:

> 취향에 맞는 AI 캐릭터를 만나고,
> 대화로 나만의 이야기를 만들어보세요.

Primary action: **캐릭터 만나보기** → `#characters`.
Secondary action: **앱 소식 받기** → `#download`, where the existing store buttons open launch-notification signup.

The former “가장 작은 연산 / 가장 오래” headline made technical and comparative claims that were not established as customer-facing benefits. The revision expresses a supported user experience, makes the AI category explicit, and previews the next action accurately. No conversion uplift claim is made; that would require measurement.

Scope: hero strings and its primary anchor / secondary icon only. Star animation and page design retained. No changes made to `/Users/hyunjoong/petty`.
